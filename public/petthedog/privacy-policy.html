Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the PetTheDog app for mobile devices, created by Nuril Baradusov as a free service.

Data Collection

The Application has no accounts and no servers, and it does not collect or transmit any personal data to us. Everything you create in it stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Camera and Photos

The camera is used to photograph the dogs you catch. Each photo is analysed on your device to find the dog and cut it out of the picture; the photo, the cutout, and anything you add (a name, an ear tag number) are stored only on your device. The Application asks to add to your photo library only when you choose to save a card you are sharing.

Location

With your permission, the Application records where each dog was caught, so you can find it again on the map. The location is stored on your device. To show a place name such as a district or city, the coordinates are sent to Apple's geocoding service; see Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles that request.

Sharing

When you share a dog, the Application makes a picture on your device and hands it to the share sheet; it goes only where you send it. The card names the district and city, never the exact coordinates.

Children's Privacy

The Application does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
